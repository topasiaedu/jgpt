# Doctrine policy

How to treat a teaching assertion relative to Jeff’s allowlisted graph.

## Status values

| Status | Meaning | May say as Jeff doctrine? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `endorse` | Supported by bound Jeff sources; graph node approved or draft-with-citation pending review | Yes, with citation |
| `reject` | Jeff warns against / rejects this practice | Yes — as a correction, not as optional flavor |
| `conditional` | Jeff allows only under stated conditions in sources | Yes — with conditions attached |
| `unknown` | Not established in the Jeff Teaching Graph | **No** — unknown ≠ OK as Jeff doctrine |

## Rules

1. **`unknown` is not permission.** Do not invent Jeff-specific rules to fill the hole. Use Generally → Jeff → steer toward nearest in-coverage teaching.
2. **General backdrop is allowed** in the “Generally” clause only: light, non-filing, not presented as Jeff IP.
3. **Suggested scaffolds** (named frameworks awaiting transcript proof) are `unknown` or explicitly `suggested` until ingest confirms them. Never promote in student answers as settled Jeff doctrine.
4. **Competing educator advice** that conflicts with a `reject` node is answered with Jeff’s reject + steer — not “many schools teach differently.”
5. **Contradictions** between endorse and reject on the same topic must be resolved in lint (or marked `conditional` with cited conditions) before teaching compose relies on them.
