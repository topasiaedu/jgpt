# Framework: Content Authority Ladder / 内容权威阶梯

**Status:** draft  
**Doctrine:** endorse (named image page in stakeholder Framework Needed PDF)  
**Priority:** elevated (`stakeholder-jeff`)

From content to compound trust. Ladder of what you are publishing.

## Levels (bottom → top, from p17 image)

1. **NEWS / 新闻**: 没观点，讲新闻
2. **POV / 观点**: 没案例，讲观点
3. **CASE / 案例**: 没故事，讲案例
4. **STORY / 故事**: 有故事，讲故事

Higher: more belongs to you, stronger trust, harder to copy.  
Lower: more external dependency, easier homogenization.
