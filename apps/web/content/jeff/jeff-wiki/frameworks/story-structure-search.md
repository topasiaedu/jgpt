# Framework: Longer story structure (Problem → Search → …)

**Status:** draft  
**Doctrine:** endorse (named on stakeholder Framework Needed PDF diagram)  
**Priority:** elevated (`stakeholder-jeff`)

Longer structure for stories (alongside short GOAT).

## Steps / structure (from diagram)

1. Problem
2. Search
3. Story
4. Solution
5. What next

PDF line: searching for the answer is what creates the story. Without the search, a story is just something that happened.
