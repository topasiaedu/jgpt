# Framework: GOAT / Four Beats

**Status:** draft  
**Doctrine:** endorse (named on stakeholder Framework Needed PDF diagram; taught in English and Chinese per note)  
**Priority:** elevated (`stakeholder-jeff`)

Short video structure most students remember.

## Steps / structure (from diagram)

1. **G Grab attention** (first three seconds)
2. **O Open a question** (make leaving costly)
3. **A Answer it** (never cheat the viewer)
4. **T Take it away** (one thing they keep)
