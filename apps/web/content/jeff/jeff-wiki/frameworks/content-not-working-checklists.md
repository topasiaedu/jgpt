# Framework: Fixing content that is not working (two checklists)

**Status:** draft  
**Doctrine:** endorse (named coach checklists in stakeholder Framework Needed PDF)  
**Priority:** elevated (`stakeholder-jeff`)

Most problems that look like filming problems are positioning problems. Two scripts for coaches.

## Checklist A: Nobody is watching (order matters)

1. **The business**: what is actually sold
2. **The method**: loud route or quiet route
3. **The words**: do customers talk like this
4. **Positioning**: usually the real answer

## Checklist B: No ideas to film

1. Fix the positioning first
2. Decide loud type or quiet type
3. Fill in the grid and their themes
4. Use the AI assistant to draft from it

PDF note: a student who has done all four has more work queued than they can film in a quarter.
