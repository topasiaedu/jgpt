# Framework: Two kinds of student, two methods

**Status:** draft  
**Doctrine:** endorse (named in stakeholder Framework Needed PDF; steps from prose + diagrams)  
**Priority:** elevated (`stakeholder-jeff`)

Loud and quiet students cannot share the same filming instructions. Extroverts get the **three C method**; introverts get the **three R method**. Same work volume; different source of material (what they think vs what they read).

DISC (D/I/S/C) appears only as a **finer coaching tune** after the loud/quiet route is set. Not treated here as a separate Jeff IP framework.

## Structure (from PDF)

1. Sort temperament first (loud / quiet).
2. Assign route: three C or three R.
3. Optionally tune coaching style with DISC under that route.
