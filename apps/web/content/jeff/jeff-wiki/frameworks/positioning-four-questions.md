# Framework: Positioning (four questions, then the crown)

**Status:** draft  
**Doctrine:** endorse (named in stakeholder Framework Needed PDF; questions from diagram)  
**Priority:** elevated (`stakeholder-jeff`)

Four questions, then the one that decides everything. Without the fourth, a business owner cannot be introduced in a sentence, and if nobody can introduce you, nobody refers you.

## Four questions → crown

1. What do you sell? (the product)
2. Who do you sell it to? (the customer)
3. Why do you sell it? (what makes you different)
4. **What are you the most of?** → **King of your own category** (narrow until number one at something real)

Ways to be the most (diagram): the fastest; the best at one named thing; the most branches; the most cases handled; the most years doing it; the only one who does it this way.

Map teaching image: you cannot get directions without telling the map where you are going.

## Two directions a personal brand answers

- **Who are you?** Standing, track record, reason to listen.
- **Why you?** What you do that nobody else does.

Students buy the second; they follow because of the first.
