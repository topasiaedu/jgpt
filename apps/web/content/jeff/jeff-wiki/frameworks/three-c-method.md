# Framework: Three C method (extroverts)

**Status:** draft  
**Doctrine:** endorse (named in stakeholder Framework Needed PDF; Cs from page diagram)  
**Priority:** elevated (`stakeholder-jeff`)

For outgoing owners who already have opinions. Permission to use conversational heat rather than tidy research-first filming.

## Steps / structure (from diagram)

1. **Controversial**: the view most people argue with
2. **Common interest**: what the whole market is discussing
3. **Conflict**: a disagreement worth having openly
4. **Take a side** and say why, in plain words
5. Outcome: content people react to (agreeing and arguing both spread it)

PDF note: risk is heat without substance; the take must be reasoned.
