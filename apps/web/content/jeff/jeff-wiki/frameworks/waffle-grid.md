# Framework: The waffle (3×3 idea grid)

**Status:** draft  
**Doctrine:** endorse (named “the waffle” in stakeholder Framework Needed PDF)  
**Priority:** elevated (`stakeholder-jeff`)

Three-by-three grid at the centre of idea generation. Middle square: who the student is **and who they serve**. Eight around: themes they own.

## Multiplication (from diagram)

5 formats (story, vlog, challenge, talking to camera, advert) × 20 to 30 themes → about **100 videos**. Same theme filmed five ways is five videos, not one.

Protects positioning: every theme must connect to the centre square.
