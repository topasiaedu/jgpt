# Framework: Three R method / routine (introverts)

**Status:** draft  
**Doctrine:** endorse (named in stakeholder Framework Needed PDF; Rs from page diagram)  
**Priority:** elevated (`stakeholder-jeff`)

For reserved owners who have substance but are uncomfortable being the source of it. Put something else in front of the camera first; material comes from outside them.

## Steps / structure (from diagram)

1. **Read the news** (any news at all)
2. **Read a book** (one chapter at a time)
3. **Read an article** (from your own industry)
4. **Add your own view** (and what you have lived)
5. Outcome: a video every day without needing a brand-new idea

## Limit (from PDF prose)

Content built entirely on other people’s material never builds authority on its own. Treat as a starting device; around month three, push more lived experience than borrowed material.
