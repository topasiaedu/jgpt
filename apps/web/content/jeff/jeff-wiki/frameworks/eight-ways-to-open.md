# Framework: Eight ways to open (first three seconds)

**Status:** draft  
**Doctrine:** endorse (named on stakeholder Framework Needed PDF diagram + prose)  
**Priority:** elevated (`stakeholder-jeff`)

Lead with the viewer’s biggest problem, not an introduction. Attention is largely decided in the first three seconds.

## Eight openings (from diagram)

1. Strong feeling
2. An argument
3. A view people fight about
4. Something everyone cares about
5. Breaking the pattern
6. A myth everyone believes
7. An unanswered question
8. Whatever is in the news

## Edit rule (from PDF)

Edit the whole video first. Find the loudest ten seconds. Move that to the front. Most students open with what they planned to say, which is almost never the best part.
