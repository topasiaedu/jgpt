# Principle: Separate advice from ego when filming IP

**Status:** draft  
**Doctrine:** endorse  
**Confidence:** 0.75

On camera, treat yourself as an actor for the IP. Don’t let ego reject team/marketer direction. Failures and corrections are usable content; shoot why customers choose you and your professional process.
