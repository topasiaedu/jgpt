## Explicit rejects / warnings

- Same instructions for loud and quiet students (completion risk).
- Content built only on other people’s material never builds authority alone (three R is a starting device).
- Opening with an introduction instead of the viewer’s biggest problem.
- Judging hits by a fixed view count instead of a follower multiple.
- Treating filming/tech problems as root cause when positioning is wrong.
- Speaking to everyone (invisible to the algorithm’s narrow groups).
- Long captions that compete with the video.
- Brand stance as beliefs only (commentators without proof work) — pending Jeff wording on the third leg.
- Internal-only lines (hiring / “hands and feet”) must not be taught as customer content.
