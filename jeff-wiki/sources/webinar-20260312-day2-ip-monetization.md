## Summary

Day 2 of a two-day free webinar. Jeff recaps Day 1 (战略篇 / 重塑商业大脑) and teaches **实战篇**: techniques, systems, and SOP so owners can build personal IP without a full production team, aiming at low-cost acquisition and sustained conversion.

Core teachings evidenced in this transcript:

1. **老板的脸就是公司的印钞机** — SME founder face/brand is the growth lever; “脸就是招牌”; people buy people.
2. **建立 IP 不需要专业团队** — owners can run a lean system / SOP (claimed: a few days per month, not daily filming).
3. **Why “用人带货”** — information gap + homogenization + trust gap (answer framed as “all of the above”); trust is a conversion key; become a visible industry leader.
4. **内容 ≠ 广告** — distinguish **内容资产** from ads; reject treating ads as content assets; learn judgment to spot and fix weak content.
5. **Not overnight fame** — explicitly not selling 一夜成名 / 爆红; for people with expertise/brand/product who have been following trends without results.
6. Day 1 depth/roadmap vs Day 2 speed/rhythm (structure of the two days).
7. Close includes enrollment CTA for offline实战 (dates/pricing in transcript — promotional, not doctrine).

Guest / case anecdotes (e.g. 华美老板 short-video outcomes) illustrate trust and story structure; treat as examples, not universal guarantees.
