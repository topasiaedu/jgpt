# Framework: What counts as a hit (100× followers)

**Status:** draft  
**Doctrine:** endorse (defined in stakeholder Framework Needed PDF; bilingual soundbite 一百倍就是爆款)  
**Priority:** elevated (`stakeholder-jeff`)

A hit is reaching **one hundred times** the student’s follower count, not a fixed view number.

## Examples (from diagram)

- 100 followers → 10,000 views
- 1,000 followers → 100,000 views
- 10,000 followers → 1,000,000 views

When one lands, turn it into a series rather than a one-off.

## Why a multiple

Fixed view targets punish small accounts. A multiple makes success available on day one and rises as the audience grows. Students who never clear the multiple have a diagnosable problem; regular clearers move to turning attention into money.
