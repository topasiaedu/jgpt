# Framework: Brand Pillars

**Status:** draft  
**Doctrine:** endorse (named on offline workshop slides; steps from OCR only)  
**Day:** D1


Four-step method to form reusable content pillars.

## Steps / structure (from slides)

1. 定义 WHO（服务谁、痛点）
2. 列10个问题（市场最常问）
3. 归类成3–5组大主题
4. 留成支柱（长期可复用栏目）
