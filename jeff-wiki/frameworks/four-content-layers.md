# Framework: THE 4 CONTENT LAYERS

**Status:** draft  
**Doctrine:** endorse (named image page in stakeholder Framework Needed PDF)  
**Priority:** elevated (`stakeholder-jeff`)

What to post when planning content.

## Four layers (from p16 image)

1. **STORY** (People connect): journey, moment, struggle, turning point, lesson
2. **CASE** (Credibility converts): proof, result, before/after, client win, testimonial
3. **POV** (Ideas differentiate): belief, insight, opinion, principle, analysis
4. **NEWS** (Relevance drives reach): trend, update, headline, event, industry news

Summary line: Lead with Story. Prove with Case. Differentiate with POV. Borrow attention from News.
