# Framework: Six caption angles (one line)

**Status:** draft  
**Doctrine:** endorse (named in stakeholder Framework Needed PDF diagram)  
**Priority:** elevated (`stakeholder-jeff`)

One line under a video because the video is the product. Long captions compete with what they should support.

## Six angles (from diagram)

1. **The sore point**: the thing they feel most
2. **The argument**: a line people will disagree with
3. **The myth**: what everyone believes and is wrong
4. **The flat statement**: said plainly, no setup
5. **The turn**: set up an expectation, break it
6. **The plain account**: today I went here and saw this

Strongest captions say the thing the reader cannot say themselves (same skill as objection tiers; tiers themselves not detailed in this PDF).
